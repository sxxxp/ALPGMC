# ALPG - Minecraft RPG Mod

Inspired by Albion Online and Path of Exile

---

## Overview

ALPG is a Minecraft RPG mod focused on progression, build crafting, and cooperative gameplay.

* Grow your mastery and define your own playstyle
* Play with friends and gain party bonuses
* Use combo-based mechanics for increased damage
* Customize builds with skill gems
* Explore dungeons and farm a wide variety of items

---

## Features

* **Classless System**
  No fixed classes. Your build defines your role.

* **Skill Gem System**
  Customize and combine skills freely.

* **Party System**
  Gain bonuses and synergy when playing with others.

* **Combo Mechanics**
  Chain skills to increase damage output.

* **Dungeons**
  Multiple dungeon types with scaling rewards.

* **Loot & Progression**
  Farm items and optimize your build.

---

## Dependencies

* Apotheosis

* Project MMO
* KubeJS
* FTB Quests

---

## Requirements

* JDK 17.0.12
* Minecraft 1.20.1
* Forge 47.2.0

---

## Getting Started

1. Install Minecraft Forge (1.20.1)
2. Install all required dependencies
3. Place the mod in the `mods` folder
4. Launch the game
