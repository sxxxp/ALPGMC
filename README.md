## Overview
A Minecraft RPG mod inspired by Albion Online and Path of Exile (ALPG).

Grow your mastery and play with friends!
Includes party bonuses and additional combo damage.
Customize your build by choosing skill gems.
Grind for a wide variety of items across diverse dungeons.

## Dependencies
- Apotheosis
- Dimensional Dungeons
- Project MMO
- KubeJS
- FTB Quests

## Versions
- JDK 17.0.12
- Minecraft 1.20.1
- Forge 47.2.0
